The rules
  • Regime. Bull when the equal-weight index of these stocks is above its 200-day average. Bear: no swing breakouts, positional risk halved.
  • Positional (3 slots, about 6 months). Buy at the open the highest-ranked stocks on 6-month momentum, distance above the 200-day average, nearness to the 52-week high and rising volume (top 20%, above the 200-day average). Stop at 3× the 20-day ATR. Time stop: 120 sessions.
  • Swing (2 slots, about 4 weeks). Bull regime only. A first new 52-week high in 60+ days on 1.5× volume: buy in the last 30 minutes. Stop at 2.5× ATR. Time stop: 20 sessions.
  • BTST (2 slots, overnight). Opens 4%+ higher on 2.5× volume and holds at or above the open: buy into the close, sell into the next day's close.
  • Exits and avoid list. Sell at the next open on a failed gap-up, a high-volume washout, a large gap-down, or a close below the stop. Then no new entry in that stock for 10 sessions (60 after a washout).
  • Sizing. Risk 1% of equity per positional trade, 0.75% per swing, 0.5% per BTST, at most 25% of equity in one stock, 5 positions in all. Costs: 0.45% round trip for midcaps, 0.75% for small caps, plus ₹16 DP charge per sale.
  • Not advice. These are the model's suggestions for your own decisions. You place every order.
